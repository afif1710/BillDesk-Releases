## ✨ Features

- 🧾 **Invoicing** — polished invoices with PDF export, partial payments, discounts, tax, and automatic overdue tracking
- ⏱️ **Time Tracking** — live timer + manual entries, billable hours, imported straight into invoices
- 💸 **Expenses & Mileage** — categorized, tax-deductible tracking with receipt attachments
- 📊 **Reports** — dashboard, Profit & Loss, revenue by client, invoice aging, and accountant-ready tax summaries
- 💱 **Multi-Currency (USD / EUR / GBP)** — set a currency per invoice and per project, and switch your dashboard totals between all three in one click
- 🌗 **Light & Dark themes**
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Private** — your data is stored locally and never leaves your computer

## ⬇️ Download & Install

1. Go to the [**latest release**](../../releases/latest) and download **`BillDesk-Setup-1.0.0.exe`**.
2. Run the installer and follow the prompts.

> ⚠️ **Windows SmartScreen** may warn that the publisher is unknown (the app is unsigned).
> Click **More info → Run anyway** to continue — this is expected and safe.

*(The `latest.yml` and `.blockmap` files in each release power in-app auto-updates — you don't need to download them manually.)*
